## Discipline & Legal Case

- **June 2024:** "卓志强接受审查调查" — placed under investigation by Guangdong's provincial discipline commission.
- **September 2024:** Publicly named in a Guangdong discipline通报 for accepting privately funded overseas travel for himself and family (2018) and borrowing a luxury car from management service targets (2018–2023).
- **November 2024:** Expelled from the CPC; retirement benefits revoked; illicit gains confiscated; case referred to prosecutors on bribery charges. The investigation found he "大搞权钱交易" — traded power for money in project contracting, administrative approvals, and personnel promotions.
