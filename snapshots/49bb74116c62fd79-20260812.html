 福建省对外贸易经济合作厅副厅长张秋 
 
 2012年04月09日 11:12     来源：中国经济网

  张秋，福建省对外贸易经济合作厅副厅长，中国贸促会福建省分会会长 
   
     张秋，男，1956年11月出生，福建省寿宁县人，中共党员，经济学硕士，现任福建省对外贸易经济合作厅党组副书记、副厅长，中国国际贸易促进委员会福建省委员会党组书记、会长，福建国际商会会长。     张秋同志曾担任福建省福州市郊区区委副书记，闽侯县县长、县委书记，福州经济技术开发区管委会主任、党委书记，马尾区区委书记；兼任福州保税区管委会主任、福州台商投资区管委会主任，福建省闽台经济贸易协会、福建省海峡商务交流协会会长。     张秋同志长期担任福建省基层党委、政府主官，并主持多个对外领域的工作，具有丰富的地方管理及对外经贸的工作经验。到福建省外经贸厅任职以来，分管过WTO事务、公平贸易、对外投资及劳务教育培训等工作；目前，主要分管投资促进、利用外资与管理、对台湾地区和港澳地区经贸合作交流等工作。2011年7月至今，担任中国国际贸易促进委员会福建省委员会党组书记、会长，福建国际商会会长。     Zhang Qiu     Deputy Director General of Fujian Provincial Department of Foreign Trade Economic Cooperation;     Chairman of CCPIT Fujian     Zhang Qiu, born on November 19, 1956, Fujian Shouning County Person, CCP party member, master of economics. He is the deputy secretary of the leading party group and deputy director general of Fujian Provincial Department of Foreign Trade Economic Cooperation, the director and chairman of the Leading Party Group of CCPIT Fujian, and the chairman of CCOIC Fujian.     Zhang Qiu has served as the deputy secretary of CPC Committee of Urban District in Fuzhou, the county mayor of Minhou County and the secretary of its CPC Committee; the director of the Administration Committee of Economic Technical Development Zone and the secretary of its CPC Committee; the secretary of CPC Committee of Mawei District in Fuzhou; he has held a concurrent post as the director of Administration Committee of Fuzhou Bonded Zone, director of Administration Committee of Fuzhou Taiwan Businessmen Investment Area, the Chairman of Fujian Provincial Fujian-Taiwan Economic Trade Association and Fujian Provincial Strait Commercial Communication Association.     Zhang Qiu has served as the chief leader of the local government and its CPC Committee for a long time, has been responsible for several foreign economic and trade fields, and has gained rich experience in local government’s management and foreign economy and trade. Since serving as the deputy director of Fujian Provincial Department of Foreign Trade Economic Cooperation, he has been in charge of WTO affairs, fair trading, foreign investment and labor education and training. At present, he is mainly responsible for investment promotion, using and managing foreign investment and the economic and trade cooperation and exchange with Taiwan, HK and Macao. From July 2011 till now, he has served as the secretary and the chairman of the Leading Party Group of CCPIT Fujian, the chairman of CCOIC Fujian.
